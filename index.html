Mobile Interior Steam Extraction & Sanitization

Daily commuting across Western Colorado introduces high levels of dust, pet dander, spilled coffee, and mountain soil into vehicle cabins. Over time, these contaminants settle deep into upholstery fibers, floor carpets, and intricate dashboard vents, degrading air quality and causing persistent cabin odors that standard vacuuming cannot resolve.

At Premium Auto Solutions, our mobile interior restoration begins with high-velocity compressed air blowouts across all instrument clusters, air conditioning ducts, seat tracks, center console seams, and dashboard crevices. This intensive dry purge dislodges fine silt and trapped grit before high-filtration commercial HEPA vacuums extract all loose soil from floorboards, seat cushions, and cargo areas.

Next, we utilize hospital-grade thermal steam cleaning machines operating above 212°F to sanitize upholstery, floor mats, and high-touch contact points. The high-temperature steam dissolves embedded beverage spills, breaks down body oils, and destroys 99.9% of bacteria and allergens without saturating underlying foam cushions or requiring harsh chemical fragrances.

The interior service concludes with nourishing leather conditioning treatments and matte-finish UV protectants applied across all vinyl, dashboards, and door panels. These protective dressings prevent drying and cracking caused by Montrose's thin, arid mountain air while leaving a non-greasy, dust-repellent finish that keeps your cabin pristine and comfortable.

Scratch-Free On-Site Exterior Hand Wash & Clay Bar

Improper washing methods and automated friction car washes are the leading causes of unsightly swirl marks, clear coat dullness, and micro-scratching on modern vehicles. Our on-site exterior detailing process uses rigorous scratch-free washing protocols designed to safeguard your paint while lifting stubborn Western Slope road grime and bug spatter.

We begin with a touchless high-lubricity foam pre-treatment that blankets your exterior panels in dense, active cleaning suds. This snow foam encapsulates abrasive dirt particles, allowing them to be safely rinsed away before any wash mitt touches the surface, eliminating the primary cause of wash-induced swirl marks.

Our technicians then conduct a meticulous two-bucket hand wash utilizing ultra-soft microfiber wash mitts and grit-guard filtration systems. We pay close attention to wheels, brake calipers, and wheel wells, applying non-acidic cleaners and specialized soft-bristle brushes to dissolve caustic brake dust and road grime safely without damaging delicate alloy finishes.

The exterior treatment is finalized with a chemical fallout decontamination and a fine-grade clay bar application. The clay bar glides smoothly over lubricated panels, shearing off microscopic industrial fallout, tree sap, and baked-on mineral residues, leaving your paint surface glass-smooth to the touch and perfectly prepped for machine polishing or protective ceramic coatings.

On-Site Paint Correction & High-Gloss Polishing

Daily driving across Montrose leaves automotive clear coats marred by light scratches, swirl marks, and hazy oxidation caused by intense Western Slope sunlight. Many vehicle owners assume that high-end machine polishing and paint correction require leaving their car at a specialty shop for multiple days, but our mobile units bring that exact restorative capability straight to your location.

Our certified mobile paint specialists begin by evaluating clear coat depth across all panels using digital ultrasonic thickness gauges. This precise measurement establishes safe polishing thresholds, ensuring that our machine correction safely levels out imperfections without compromising factory clear coat durability.

Utilizing precision dual-action random orbital machine polishers paired with diminishing micro-abrasive compounds and specialized pads, we methodically polish the paint in targeted sections. This systematic process levels out microscopic imperfections, eliminating surface haze, buffer trails, and wash scratches while ensuring zero risk of unsightly holograms.

At Premium Auto Solutions, our on-site paint correction safely eliminates between 80% and 95%+ of surface clear coat defects, revealing unmatched optical clarity, intense color richness, and a stunning mirror-like reflection. The resulting finish provides the ultimate foundation for enduring gloss and makes long-term surface protection substantially more effective.

Mobile Ceramic Coating & Paint Sealant Applications

Western Colorado's intense high-altitude UV rays, blowing alkaline soil, and winter road chemicals rapidly degrade conventional waxes and synthetic spray sealants. Drivers seeking long-term surface defense can now enjoy professional-grade ceramic coatings installed conveniently at their home or workplace.

Our mobile technicians apply commercial ceramic coatings either inside your residential garage or under specialized mobile canopies that control ambient temperature, dust, and direct sunlight. The coating chemically bonds with factory clear coats at the molecular level, forming a semi-permanent, 9H-rated nano-crystalline glass barrier.

The resulting ceramic surface delivers exceptional hydrophobic and oleophobic performance. Water, road slush, tree sap, and bird droppings bead up and slide off effortlessly, keeping your automobile cleaner for significantly longer periods and making routine driveway washes fast and effortless.

In addition to superior stain resistance, our ceramic coatings integrate advanced ultraviolet inhibitors that prevent clear coat oxidation, hazing, and color fading over multiple years. Experience permanent gloss, unmatched surface resilience, and the convenience of professional mobile installation right in Montrose.
