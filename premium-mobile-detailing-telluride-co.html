High-Altitude Interior Steam Sanitation & Leather Therapy

Telluride's arid mountain air, frequent temperature fluctuations, and dusty backcountry roads take a severe toll on vehicle interiors. Fine decomposed granite, ski traction grit, dried mud, and seasonal pine needles quickly embed into carpets and upholstery fibers, creating micro-abrasions and degrading cabin air quality beyond the reach of standard vacuums.

At Premium Auto Solutions, our mobile interior restoration begins with intensive high-velocity compressed air purging across every seam, air vent, instrument dial, seat track, and under-seat recess. This dry extraction lifts stubborn grit and organic matter before commercial-grade HEPA filtration vacuums thoroughly extract loose particulate from carpets, floor mats, and trunk compartments.

Next, we administer hospital-grade thermal steam cleaning operating above 212°F across all contact points, footwells, center consoles, and door panels. The high-temperature steam safely sanitizes surfaces, dissolves dried beverage stains, lifts salt crusts from ski boots, and eliminates 99.9% of bacteria and allergens without introducing harsh fumes or over-saturating underlying padding.

To preserve fine luxury upholstery, we perform specialized pH-balanced leather cleansing followed by deep penetrating conditioning treatments formulated with natural essential oils and UV inhibitors. This crucial therapy replenishes natural moisture in Nappa, semi-aniline, and Merino leathers, preventing high-altitude cracking, premature shrinkage, and fading caused by Telluride's intense solar radiation, delivered with white-glove care via Premium Mobile Detailing Telluride, CO.

Scratch-Free Alpine Hand Wash & Salt Decontamination

Navigating mountain roads during Telluride winters coats vehicles in a caustic slurry of magnesium chloride, coarse traction sand, and road brine. When washed improperly or run through automated mechanical brushes, these sharp abrasive residues inflict severe swirl marks, clear coat scratches, and dullness across exterior paintwork.

Our on-site exterior wash protocol starts with a touchless high-lubricity foam pre-soak that envelops your vehicle in rich, active suds. The specialized foam breaks the ionic bond between corrosive road chemicals and clear coat finishes, encapsulating sharp abrasive grit so it can be safely rinsed away without mechanical friction.

Technicians then perform a delicate two-bucket hand wash using ultra-plush microfiber wash mitts and grit-guard filtration buckets. Every wheel barrel, brake caliper, and fender liner receives intensive agitation with non-acidic wheel cleaners and ultra-soft detailing brushes to dissolve sintered brake dust and caked-on mountain grime safely.

The exterior wash is completed with a chemical iron fallout neutralizer and a fine-grade clay bar treatment lubricated with high-slickness polymers. The clay bar gently shears away bonded tree sap, industrial fallout, and embedded road debris, restoring an ultra-smooth, glass-like finish that is ideally primed for polishing and protective coatings with Premium Mobile Detailing Telluride, CO.

On-Site Multi-Stage Paint Correction & Gloss Restoration

Intense high-altitude sunshine in the San Juan Mountains highlights every paint flaw, from automated car wash swirls and trail brush scuffs to chemical water spots and buffer hazing. Vehicle owners often believe that precision paint correction requires an extended stay at a specialty body shop, but our mobile units bring that identical high-caliber restoration right to your garage.

At Premium Auto Solutions, our certified paint specialists begin with an ultrasonic digital paint depth analysis across every exterior panel. By measuring clear coat thickness, we calculate precise safety margins, guaranteeing that our machine polishing permanently levels clear coat imperfections without compromising the protective factory clear coat layer.

Utilizing state-of-the-art dual-action orbital machine polishers paired with diminishing micro-abrasives and specialized foam or microfiber pads, we systematically correct the paint in controlled sections. This multi-stage process eliminates surface haze, buffer trails, and wash scratches while ensuring zero risk of unsightly holograms.

Our on-site paint correction safely removes 85% to 95%+ of surface defects, restoring remarkable optical depth, mirror-like clarity, and vibrant color saturation. Mobile Ceramic Coating & Long-Term Alpine Defense

Telluride’s high elevation subjects vehicle clear coats to 30% to 40% more ultraviolet radiation than sea level, accelerating oxidation, clear coat hazing, and pigment bleaching. Conventional carnauba waxes and synthetic sealants degrade within weeks under extreme thermal cycling and heavy winter deicers, demanding a permanent nano-ceramic barrier.

Our mobile technicians apply commercial-grade ceramic coatings inside your private garage or under specialized portable clean-air canopies that shield panels from dust and direct sunlight. The ceramic formula cross-links molecularly with the clear coat, curing into an impenetrable 9H-rated glass shield.

The resulting nano-ceramic surface provides intense hydrophobic and oleophobic properties. Water, slush, snowmelt, and caustic magnesium chloride bead tightly and roll off effortlessly, preventing corrosive road chemicals from bonding to your paint and making routine washes effortless and scratch-free.

Furthermore, our ceramic coatings incorporate robust UV-blocking nanoparticles that prevent clear coat fading, oxidation, and mineral etching for years. Experience lasting showroom gloss, maximum resale protection, and the unrivaled convenience of professional on-site installation anywhere in the region with Premium Mobile Detailing Telluride, CO.
